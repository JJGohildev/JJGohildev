## Selected projects

### British Airways Reviews
**Which service areas received the strongest and weakest ratings?**

Analyzed **901 historical reviews** in Tableau. Cabin staff received the highest average service rating, while entertainment scored lower across a substantial sample. Response counts help put the comparisons in context.

[View interactive dashboard](https://public.tableau.com/app/profile/jaydipsinh.gohil/viz/BritishAirwaysReviewsDashboard_17889618499830/OverviewTrends) · [View project](https://github.com/JJGohildev/ba-reviews-tableau-dashboard)

### Customer Experience Analytics
**How do support demand and customer satisfaction change over time?**

Built a Power BI report covering ticket volume, satisfaction, response times, and resolution. The project includes SQL validation checks and documented KPI definitions.

[View project and screenshots](https://github.com/JJGohildev/cx-analytics-dashboard)

### HR Performance and Reporting
**How does the workforce change, and which reporting steps can be automated?**

Built Power BI views of headcount, attrition, engagement, and training, alongside an Excel VBA routine designed to refresh workbook data and export a PDF summary.

[View project and screenshots](https://github.com/JJGohildev/HR-Performance-KPI-dashboard)

*The customer experience and HR projects use simulated data for fictional companies.*
